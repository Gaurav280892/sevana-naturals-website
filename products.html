Portfolio

The oils we trade.

A focused selection of Indian essential oils, attars, and aromatic raw materials — organised by botanical family and end‑use application.

I · The mint family

Mentha — the heart of the trade.

India produces the majority of the world's natural menthol, and the Bareilly‑Chandausi‑Sambhal corridor is the centre of that trade. Our mint family supply runs through three generations of farmer and distiller relationships in this belt.

  • Mentha arvensis Mentha arvensis L.

    Cornmint oil — the source material for natural menthol. Crystallisable, high‑menthol grades available, as well as DMO (de‑mentholised oil) for flavour and fragrance applications.

    Steam distilled Flavour grade Fragrance grade
  • Mentha piperita Mentha × piperita L.

    Peppermint oil — cooler, sweeter, with elevated menthone and menthofuran versus arvensis. Sought for oral care, confectionery, and fine fragrance compositions.

    Steam distilled Flavour grade
  • Mentha spicata Mentha spicata L.

    Spearmint oil — carvone‑dominant, characteristic warm‑sweet mint profile. A staple of chewing gum, toothpaste, and confectionery applications globally.

    Steam distilled Flavour grade
  • Menthol crystals L‑menthol, USP/BP grade

    Natural L‑menthol crystals isolated from Mentha arvensis — the form most widely traded into oral care, pharmaceutical, and confectionery manufacture.

    USP grade BP grade Pharmaceutical

II · Aromatic grasses

Citronella, lemongrass, palmarosa.

Steam‑distilled at scale from cultivated plantations across the Tarai belt and Tamil Nadu, with consistent citral, geraniol, and citronellal profiles for fragrance and flavour applications.

  • Citronella oil Cymbopogon winterianus Jowitt

    Java‑type citronella oil — rich in citronellal and geraniol, the working substrate for hydroxycitronellal and a foundational note in functional fragrance compositions and household applications.

    Steam distilled Fragrance grade
  • Lemongrass oil Cymbopogon flexuosus (East Indian)

    East Indian lemongrass oil — high‑citral content for the manufacture of synthetic citral, ionones, and Vitamin A precursors, and a citrus‑floral note in fragrance and flavour applications.

    Steam distilled 75–85% citral
  • Palmarosa oil Cymbopogon martinii var. motia

    Geraniol‑rich rose‑like grass oil, traditionally used as a cost‑effective extender or alternative in rose‑family fragrance compositions and as a substrate in geraniol isolation.

    Steam distilled Fragrance grade

III · Specialty naturals

Where Indian origin is irreplaceable.

A curated selection of oils and absolutes where India is either the sole or the leading commercial source — and where authenticity, provenance, and aging matter more than price.

  • Ruh Khus Chrysopogon zizanioides (North Indian wild)

    Wild‑harvested North Indian vetiver, hydro‑distilled from grass that grows along the seasonal floodplains of the Ganga and the Ramganga. Levorotatory, sesquiterpene‑rich, with the deep earthy‑smoky‑sweet character that defines true ruh khus. Fresh and aged stock available.

    Wild harvest Hydro distilled Aged stock available
  • Davana absolute Artemisia pallens Wall. ex DC.

    A specialty Indian aromatic with no commercial production outside the subcontinent. Davanone‑rich, fruity‑woody‑herbal profile prized by niche perfumers and the world's leading fragrance houses for its uniqueness.

    India exclusive Fragrance grade
  • Tagetes oil Tagetes minuta L.

    Marigold oil from the Kumaon hills — herbaceous, fruity, with a characteristic tagetone profile. IFRA‑restricted, supplied with full documentation for compliant compositions.

    Steam distilled IFRA compliant

IV · Attars & absolutes

From Kannauj.

A short drive south of Bareilly is Kannauj — India's only city built entirely around perfumery, where the deg‑bhapka tradition of hydro‑distillation has continued unbroken for centuries. Our attars and floral absolutes are sourced directly from Kannauj master distillers.

  • Rose attar (Kannauj) Rosa damascena on sandalwood base

    Traditional deg‑bhapka attar — Damask rose distilled directly onto Indian sandalwood oil as the receiving medium. The result is unlike any rose oil, absolute, or accord made elsewhere.

    Deg‑bhapka Kannauj
  • Jasmine sambac Jasminum sambac (L.) Aiton

    Concrete and absolute, from Tamil Nadu and Uttar Pradesh cultivation. A core natural in Indian and international fine perfumery, with a depth that synthetic equivalents have never replicated.

    Concrete Absolute
  • Tuberose absolute Polianthes tuberosa L.

    Solvent‑extracted absolute from South Indian flowers. Creamy, narcotic, white‑floral. One of the most expensive florals in perfumery, and one of the most valued.

    Absolute
  • Mitti attar Petrichor — baked earth distillate

    A uniquely Indian attar: the scent of the first rains on dry earth, captured by distilling baked clay over a sandalwood base. Cultural artefact and modern perfumery curiosity in one bottle.

    Deg‑bhapka Kannauj
