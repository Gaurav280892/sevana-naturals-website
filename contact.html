Sheetal Sachan is a fragrance developer with formal training across three European institutions and four years in the commercial fragrance industry.

At Sterling Perfumes Industries in Dubai, she served as Fine Fragrance Evaluator & Developer from 2022 to 2024, reporting directly to the CEO. In that role she led the olfactive development of more than five hundred fine fragrance launches across the Middle East and global markets — for brands including ARMAF, Club de Nuit, Hamidi, Flavia, Georgians, Just Jack, Jenny Glow, SaOud Alkabi, and the Estiara line — working at the interface between Sterling and the world's leading fragrance houses, including Firmenich, Givaudan, and IFF.

She began her industry career as a fragrance evaluator at V. Mane Fils, Dubai, evaluating cross‑category fragrance briefs for the GCC, Levant, and Pakistani markets. Her academic foundation combines a European Fragrances & Cosmetic Master from ISIPCA Versailles — the world's leading institution in perfumery and cosmetic education — a Master in Business & Management from the University of Padova, and a Bachelor of Technology in Biotechnology and Bioinformatics with a peer‑reviewed research publication.

Sevana Naturals is her return to the family trade. She grew up alongside the work that her father and brother‑in‑law have built in Bareilly. After three years applying European perfumery standards in the Gulf, she returned to India to bring those standards back to the source.

The family trade

Bareilly, since the early 1990s.

The Patel family has been distilling, trading, and exporting essential oils from Uttar Pradesh for more than three decades.

i.

Sona Chemicals

Founded by Yadvendra Patel, 1990s — Bareilly, U.P.

The senior family house in the trade, established by Sheetal's father over thirty years ago. Sona Chemicals built the procurement relationships and farmer networks that now anchor Sevana's supply chain — across mentha, citronella, lemongrass, and the wild‑harvested khus grass of the North Indian river plains.

ii.

Sona Mint Overseas

Run by Prateek Patel — Bareilly, U.P.

The allied family house, focused on the mentha and menthol export trade. Operates from the same Bareilly base and shares the family network of distillation partners. Sona Mint Overseas continues to serve its established trade; Sevana opens a parallel channel for specialty oils and the wider perfumery market.

§

Bareilly sits at the centre of India's mint belt — the Bareilly‑Chandausi‑Sambhal‑Rampur corridor that produces the majority of the world's natural menthol. The same belt, with its alluvial soils and groundwater fed by the Ganga and the Ramganga, also supports the wild khus grass that yields ruh khus, the citronella and lemongrass plantations of the Tarai region, and — a short drive south — Kannauj, India's only city built entirely around perfumery.

Sevana Naturals is the work of inheriting this geography deliberately. We are not new to the trade. We are new to bringing this trade to the standard the rest of the world's perfumery industry expects.

Our approach

Olfactive standard. Analytical proof.

We do three things differently from a conventional Indian essential oils trader.

i

A perfumer evaluates every lot.

Before any oil is committed for shipment, it is smelt, judged, and assessed against our internal olfactive library and the standard our clients formulate to. Five hundred launches at Sterling left a calibrated nose. We use it on every batch.

ii

Lab analysis at scientific institutions.

Our lots are tested at CSIR‑CIMAP Lucknow and the Fragrance & Flavour Development Centre at Kannauj — the two Indian institutions that wrote the analytical standards for the oils we trade. Not third‑party commercial labs.

iii

Storage that does not compromise.

Food‑grade stainless steel drums, nitrogen‑flushed headspace, PTFE‑gasketed seals, dark and temperature‑stable storage. No aluminium. No plastic for aging stock. The standard is the same as the European houses we have shipped to before, applied here.
